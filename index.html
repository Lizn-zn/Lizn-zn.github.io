01About

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Computer Science at ETH Zürich, working with Prof. Zhendong Su. I obtained my Ph.D. from Nanjing University under the supervision of Prof. Xiaoxing Ma and Prof. Jian Lü, and my B.Sc. in Mathematics from Southwest Jiaotong University.

Research perspective

Before the agent era. My previous work spanned trustworthy machine learning, neuro-symbolic learning, and formal reasoning. It covered neural network testing, calibration and conformal prediction; learning with logical constraints and mathematical reasoning; and autoformalization, theorem proving, and verified code generation. I also worked on software engineering automation and minimax/bilevel optimization. Across these topics, I explored how learning, symbolic structure, and rigorous evaluation can support reliable computation.

In the agent era. I am now turning to generic verification: how to check whether an agent’s reasoning, actions, and outputs satisfy a task’s requirements. I am interested in verification methods that generalize across tasks and environments, beyond theorem proving and code verification. My aim is to make verification a general capability that agents can use to assess progress, detect errors, and guide their next steps.
